The arc we can tell

From experimentation
to maturity.

Tell the project story from non-forks.

Use forks to investigate possible upstream work, but do not count a fork as Amadeus-led activity without evidence of contribution, stewardship or publication.

16 / The evidence contract

Make confidence visible

Every claim gets
a label.

Observed

Captured directly

GitHub API, repository README, public documentation or Board Project field at a stated date.

Reported

Supplied material

Draft report, key-ideas document or stakeholder-provided number that still needs refresh ownership.

Interpreted

Narrative synthesis

A human-readable conclusion built from multiple sources; useful, but not a new measurement.

Pending

Needs a decision

Publication approval, project owner, legal/IP check, current metric or external validation.

Scope

Say what is counted

Repository population, fork rule, team cohort, time window and custom status vocabulary.

Refresh

Make it repeatable

Keep the command, capture date and raw artifact beside the deck or report.

This contract is recorded in `.memories/evidence-ledger.md` and is the recommended basis for future public reporting.
